Cloud + AI Workflow

Recommendation publishing pipeline

PulseCue models how a cloud-ready content platform could receive audience events, score catalog items, prepare AI-assisted explanations, and route approved recommendations into product, email, learning, or streaming surfaces.

1 Audience Signal API Ratings, completion, clicks, and segment intent enter the scoring layer.
2 AI Recommendation Engine Affinity, engagement, freshness, cost, and business goal signals are combined.
3 Human Review Gate Teams approve sensitive, strategic, or high-impact recommendations.
4 Cloud Publishing Queue Approved content is ready for API delivery, campaign export, or product placement.
